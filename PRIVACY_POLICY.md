# XMusic Privacy Policy

Last updated: <12/11/2026>

## 1. Who we are
XMusic ("the bot", "we") is a Discord music bot operated by <bon>.
Contact: <Discord: tann2.6.02>

## 2. What data we handle
XMusic only responds to slash commands and button interactions. It does **not** read, log or store the content of regular messages, direct messages or member profiles.

The data we handle is limited to:
- **Server (guild) ID** and the **voice / text channel IDs** used by the bot (for the music panel and the 24/7 channel)
- **User ID** of a member who runs a command (to check permissions and to show who requested a track)
- **Per-server settings**: volume, loop mode, shuffle state, audio effect, queue contents, and the 24/7 channel
- **Track information** (title, link) of songs added to the queue

## 3. How we use it
Only to operate the bot's features inside your server. We do not sell data, share it for advertising, build user profiles, or use it to train AI models.

## 4. Storage and security
Data is stored in local files on the server that runs the bot. Access is limited to the bot's operator. Bot credentials (tokens, API keys) are kept private and are never published.

## 5. Retention and deletion
Per-server data is kept only while the bot is in that server. When the bot is removed from a server, that server's data is deleted. You can also ask for deletion at any time by contacting us.

## 6. Third-party services
Music links and searches are resolved through YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud and TikTok. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to that content. Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply to your use of the bot.

## 7. Children
XMusic is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below it.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
